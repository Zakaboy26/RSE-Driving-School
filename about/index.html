About us

Friendly, personalised lessons that build safe drivers for life.

Our approach

Road Safety Expert Driving School was founded by Atta Ur Rahman, out of a passion for teaching safe driving. He saw the need for a school that put personalised instruction and student-centred learning first.

We focus on more than passing your test. Our goal is a confident, safe and independent driver. Lessons are adapted to your pace, strengths and areas for improvement, with calm, clear communication.
