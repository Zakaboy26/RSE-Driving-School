Practical driving test

What to expect on test day.

This is a plain-English overview. For the full, current rules see GOV.UK.

Parts of the test

  • An eyesight check (failing it ends the test).
  • Two vehicle safety questions: Show Me, Tell Me.
  • General driving ability, including independent driving.
  • One reversing manoeuvre.

Faults

Minor mistakes are driving faults and do not on their own fail the test. Serious or dangerous faults do fail it. The examiner stops the test only if they think your driving is a danger to others.

Preparing

Test routes are not published, so you cannot memorise them. Learn to observe and decide for yourself on any road. Practise regularly, take a mock test if you can, and ask your instructor for honest feedback. Nothing can guarantee a pass, but good preparation helps you feel calm.
