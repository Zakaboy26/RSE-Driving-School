How the test works, and an unofficial practice quiz.

How the test works

The car theory test is multiple choice with 50 questions, and you need at least 43 correct to pass. There is also a separate hazard perception part. Check the current format on GOV.UK before you book.

Revise the Highway Code, not just practice answers. Understanding why a rule exists makes the answer easier to work out. When a question seems unclear, look for key words and rule out answers that are clearly unsafe.
