Lessons and services

Prices are available on request. Please call or email.

Manual lessons

Learn in a car with a clutch and gears. Suits anyone who wants a licence that covers both manual and automatic cars.

Suitable for: Beginners, returners and learners wanting the full licence.

What to expect: Clear step-by-step coaching on clutch control, gear changes and busy-road decisions at your own pace.

Enquire about this

Automatic lessons

Learn without a clutch, so you can focus on observation, road positioning and decision-making.

Suitable for: Learners who want a simpler start, or who plan to drive an automatic.

What to expect: Lessons in an automatic car. Note that an automatic test pass only covers automatic cars.

Enquire about this

Beginner lessons

A calm start for people who have never driven. We begin with the controls and build up gradually.

Suitable for: Complete beginners of any age.

What to expect: Quiet practice areas first, then residential roads as your confidence grows.

Enquire about this

Refresher lessons

Rebuild confidence after a break, or after moving to the UK.

Suitable for: Licence holders who feel rusty or nervous.

What to expect: A first lesson to see where you are, then a plan to work on the areas you choose.

Enquire about this

Mock driving tests

A practice run under test-style conditions so test day feels familiar.

Suitable for: Learners close to test standard.

What to expect: A test-style drive, followed by honest feedback on what to improve.

Enquire about this

Test day car hire

Use the instructor's car for your practical test, including a pre-test warm-up.

Suitable for: Learners booked for a practical test.

What to expect: A warm-up drive before the test, in a car you already know. Contact us to confirm availability and cost.

Enquire about this

Pass Plus

Extra training for new drivers who want more experience in different conditions. Some insurers may offer a discount, but this is not guaranteed.

Suitable for: Drivers who have recently passed.

What to expect: Contact us to confirm what the course covers and how it is arranged.

Enquire about this

Theory test support

Guidance on how to revise for the theory test, with short theory chats linked to real situations during lessons.

Suitable for: Anyone preparing for the theory test.

What to expect: Revision advice, multiple-choice technique and links to our free Learning Centre.

Enquire about this

Nervous learner support

Lessons with a patient, calm approach and clear communication.

Suitable for: Anyone anxious about driving.

What to expect: You set the pace. We talk through each step before you do it.
