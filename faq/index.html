Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Call or email us.

Do you offer manual and automatic lessons?

Yes, we offer both manual and automatic lessons.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. Many of our learners have never driven before. We start with the basics and move at your pace.

Do I need to pass my theory test before taking driving lessons?

No. You need a provisional licence to start lessons, but you do not have to pass the theory test first. You do need to pass it before you can book your practical test.

How many lessons might I need?

It varies from person to person. It depends on your starting point, how often you practise and how confident you feel. Your instructor will give you an honest view as you progress.

How often should I take lessons?

Regular lessons help you remember what you have learned. Many learners find one or two a week works well. We can discuss what suits your schedule.

Do you offer refresher lessons?

Yes. See our services page, or contact us to talk about what you need.

Do you teach nervous learners?

Yes. We aim for calm, stress-free lessons with clear communication.

Can you help me prepare for my practical test?

Yes. We offer test preparation and mock driving tests, and our Learning Centre explains how the test works.

Can I use your car for my driving test?

Yes, test day car hire is available, including a pre-test warm-up. Contact us to confirm availability and cost.

Can you help me book a driving test?

Yes, contact us and we can discuss how we can help with test bookings.

What happens if it rains?

Lessons normally go ahead in rain, and learning to drive in different weather is useful. Contact your instructor if you have concerns about severe weather.

What should I bring to my first lesson?

Bring your provisional driving licence. If you wear glasses or contact lenses to drive, bring them too. Contact us if you are unsure about anything else.

Can I be picked up from home, work or college?

Pick-up arrangements depend on the area and availability. Contact us with your postcode and we will confirm.

Do you offer intensive courses?

Contact us to ask about availability and what we can arrange.

Do you offer motorway lessons?

Contact us to ask about motorway tuition. Learners may use motorways only with an approved driving instructor in a car with dual controls.

Do you provide Pass Plus?

Yes, Pass Plus training is available for new drivers who want more experience.
